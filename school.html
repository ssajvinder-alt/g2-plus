From the Principal’s desk

A welcome to our school community.

Sh. Vinod Kumar · Principal

Proposed welcome message · awaiting the Principal’s approval

Dear students, parents, and members of our community, welcome to Government Senior Secondary School, Sito Gunno. Our school website brings together useful information about our staff, student enrolment, and learning resources.

Education is strengthened by regular study, curiosity, discipline, and respect for others. I encourage every student to ask questions, develop good habits, and approach each new challenge with confidence. Through cooperation between school and home, we can support students in preparing for the next stage of their lives.

Let us work together to make learning purposeful and our school community caring and responsible.
